Recorded by Aomi Labs: [Prepare an ETH Bridge to Base and Keep a Gas Buffer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTFiRHuEKTk).

<Video src="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTFiRHuEKTk" title="Prepare an ETH Bridge to Base and Keep a Gas Buffer" />

This is a silent product recording. The prompt and responses are visible on screen. It shows balance checks, route preparation and the simulated transaction staged for wallet approval.

Aomi's own [docs introduction](https://aomi.dev/docs) uses the same example request: "Move most of my ETH over to Base, but leave enough on mainnet for gas." Across handles the bridge and preserves a gas buffer.

## Try it yourself

Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet. Use Ethereum as the starting network, then ask Aomi to move most of the ETH to Base while leaving enough on mainnet for gas.

Amounts, routes and quotes in the recording are examples. Yours will differ, so read the simulation before you sign.
