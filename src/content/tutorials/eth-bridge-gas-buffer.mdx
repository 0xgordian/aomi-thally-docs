This page is based on the official video title and description, plus Aomi's public docs. I could not get captions for this recording, so the exact on-screen clicks were not independently verified.

The official description says this is a silent product recording, and that the prompt and responses are visible on screen. It shows balance checks, route preparation and the simulated transaction staged for wallet approval.

Aomi's own [docs introduction](https://aomi.dev/docs) uses the same example request: "Move most of my ETH over to Base, but leave enough on mainnet for gas." Across handles the bridge and preserves a gas buffer.


## How to try this in the portal

These steps come from the [Portal](https://aomi.dev/docs/trade/portal) guide.

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev).
2. Connect a wallet or sign in when you are ready to transact.
3. Set the network in the header to the chain used in the recording.
4. Type the same kind of request the video describes.
5. Review the simulation in the chat, then sign in your wallet only if you want to send it. Rejecting the prompt does nothing onchain.


Use Ethereum as the starting network, then ask Aomi to move most of the ETH to Base while leaving enough on mainnet for gas. I did not re-run this bridge or confirm the route shown in the recording.
