Recorded by Aomi Labs: [Transact with Aomi - ENS Registration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_T7wnsHMjbY).

<Video src="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_T7wnsHMjbY" title="Transact with Aomi, ENS Registration" />

What the recording covers:

- Fetch availability of aomi.eth from ENS
- Interact with the ENS controller for 1 year of registration
- Commit to the registry with a transaction (didn't register...)

The last line is important: the recording commits, and the recording stops before registration completes.

To explore a similar flow today, ask Aomi at [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) to check an ENS name and, if you intend to register, review every transaction before signing. Don't assume `aomi.eth` is still available.
