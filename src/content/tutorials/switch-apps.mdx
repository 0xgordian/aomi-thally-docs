Official video: [Transact with Aomi - Switching agentic applications](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSXkgjIfuKk).

<Video src="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSXkgjIfuKk" title="Transact with Aomi, Switching agentic applications" />

This page is based on the official video title and description, plus Aomi's public docs. I could not get captions for this recording, so the exact on-screen clicks were not independently verified.

The official description says Aomi apps are a stateless definition of prompt, tools and workflows, and that this defines what the assistant does. The video switches from:

- Default app: a total of 11 tools that execute with protocols on EVM
- DeFi app: integrates trading APIs from 0x, CowSwap and LiFi in addition to the defaults

The description is cut off after that, so I am not adding a third app that I cannot see.

The current [Portal](https://aomi.dev/docs/trade/portal) guide describes the same idea with today's UI:

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev).
2. Use the app selector in the composer to scope the thread to one App, or let the agent route across installed packages.
3. Use the packages catalog to browse and install Apps.

Tool counts and the exact 0x / CowSwap / LiFi bundle are from the recording, not something I re-counted in today's catalog.
