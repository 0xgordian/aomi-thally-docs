## What the video and description show

FanForge is an Early Forge project built on Aomi. It lets a creator launch a creator coin from one Telegram message, without wallet setup, jargon or prior crypto experience.

In the recording, the presenter starts the FanForge bot and shows a Telegram chat. Asking "who are you?" gets a reply from Aomi's default agent, then the demo continues inside that Telegram conversation.

## How a similar Telegram app is set up today

Public docs for putting an Aomi App on Telegram are at [Put your App on Telegram](https://aomi.dev/docs/build/telegram):

1. Deploy an App from [build.aomi.dev](https://build.aomi.dev) or the [first App guide](/guides/first-app).
2. Follow the Telegram guide to turn that deployed App into a hosted Telegram bot.
3. Users can then talk to the bot in Telegram. Signing, when it is needed, still happens with a user-controlled wallet. The [Aomi on Telegram](/tutorials/telegram) clip states that Aomi never touches the private key.

FanForge was an Early Forge demo. The Telegram setup on Aomi Build may look different today.
