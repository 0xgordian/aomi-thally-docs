Recorded by Aomi Labs: [Transact with Aomi - connect wallet to sign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqCiJ7m9ksY).

<Video src="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TqCiJ7m9ksY" title="Transact with Aomi, connect wallet to sign" />

A custodial wallet is not always necessary to transact with AI. Aomi only builds your transaction. You sign off as human-in-the-loop.

That matches the current [Portal](/guides/portal) model:

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev).
2. Connect a wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or WalletConnect on EVM; a wallet-adapter wallet on Solana) or sign in with email or Google for an embedded wallet.
3. Ask for a transaction in plain English.
4. Review the simulation, then sign in your wallet. Aomi does not hold the key.
