This page is based on the official video title and description, plus Aomi's public docs. I could not get captions for this recording, so the exact on-screen clicks were not independently verified.

The official description says a custodial wallet is not always necessary to transact with AI. Aomi only builds your transaction. You sign off as human-in-the-loop.

That matches the current [Portal](https://aomi.dev/docs/trade/portal) model:

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev).
2. Connect a wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or WalletConnect on EVM; a wallet-adapter wallet on Solana) or sign in with email or Google for an embedded wallet.
3. Ask for a transaction in plain English.
4. Review the simulation, then sign in your wallet. Aomi does not hold the key.
