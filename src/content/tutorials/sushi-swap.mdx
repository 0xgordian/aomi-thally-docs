Recorded by Aomi Labs: [Transact with Aomi - 10 USDT Swap on Sushi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aZv1zHtQXb4).

<Video src="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aZv1zHtQXb4" title="Transact with Aomi, 10 USDT Swap on Sushi" />

Aomi transacts on the public blockchain without APIs, CEX or DEX endpoints, aggregators, SDKs or hard-coded routes. The runtime interfaces with the chain itself, on top of a blockchain node. The recorded action is a 10 USDT swap on Sushi.

To try a similar swap today, use the [Portal](/guides/portal):

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet.
2. Set the network for the thread.
3. Ask Aomi to swap 10 USDT on Sushi, or to swap 10 USDT and let it choose a route.
4. Review the simulation, then sign only if you want to send it.
