This page is based on the official video title and description, plus Aomi's public docs. I could not get captions for this recording, so the exact on-screen clicks were not independently verified.

The official description says Aomi transacts on the public blockchain without APIs, CEX or DEX endpoints, aggregators, SDKs or hard-coded routes. The runtime interfaces with the chain itself, on top of a blockchain node. The recorded action is a 10 USDT swap on Sushi.

To try a similar swap today, use the [Portal](https://aomi.dev/docs/trade/portal):

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet.
2. Set the network for the thread.
3. Ask Aomi to swap 10 USDT on Sushi, or to swap 10 USDT and let it choose a route.
4. Review the simulation, then sign only if you want to send it.

I did not re-run this swap, and I cannot confirm from captions which chain or output token was used.
