A recorded Aomi session starts with one request: split 50 USDG equally across a basket of three stock tokens. Aomi resolves assets on Robinhood Chain and prepares the swap batch and approval steps. The video demonstrates transaction preparation. It does not establish that every trade settled.

## Try it yourself

Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet. If Robinhood Chain is in the network picker, select it, then ask Aomi to split an amount of USDG equally across three stock tokens. Review the prepared batch and approvals before signing.

Amounts, routes and quotes in the recording are examples. Yours will differ, so read the simulation before you sign.
