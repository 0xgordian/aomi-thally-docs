A plain-English request becomes a prepared batch of stock-token swaps and approvals on Robinhood Chain. The clip shows transaction preparation, not proof that every trade settled.

This is a shorter companion to [Build a tokenized stock basket](/tutorials/stock-basket-robinhood).

## Try it yourself

Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet. Select Robinhood Chain if it is available, then ask Aomi to build a stock-token basket from one request and review the prepared approvals.

Amounts, routes and quotes in the recording are examples. Yours will differ, so read the simulation before you sign.
