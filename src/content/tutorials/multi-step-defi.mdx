This page is based on the official video title and description, plus Aomi's public docs. I could not get captions for this recording, so the exact on-screen clicks were not independently verified.

The official description says the recording follows a DeFi sequence involving Lido, Aave and token transfers. Aomi asks for missing scheduling details and works through the requested actions. The clip does not demonstrate a recurring job running over time. It is a silent screen recording of the planning and action flow.

The [docs introduction](https://aomi.dev/docs) has a related example, "Yes, do the whole sequence," where Lido, Aave and ERC-20 steps persist as a recurring intent. This video, by its own description, does not show that recurring job actually running.


## How to try this in the portal

These steps come from the [Portal](https://aomi.dev/docs/trade/portal) guide.

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev).
2. Connect a wallet or sign in when you are ready to transact.
3. Set the network in the header to the chain used in the recording.
4. Type the same kind of request the video describes.
5. Review the simulation in the chat, then sign in your wallet only if you want to send it. Rejecting the prompt does nothing onchain.


Ask Aomi to plan a multi-step sequence involving Lido, Aave and a token transfer. If it asks for scheduling details, answer from what you actually want. I did not re-run this sequence.
