The recording follows a DeFi sequence involving Lido, Aave and token transfers. Aomi asks for missing scheduling details and works through the requested actions. The clip does not demonstrate a recurring job running over time. It is a silent screen recording of the planning and action flow.

The [docs introduction](https://aomi.dev/docs) has a related example, "Yes, do the whole sequence," where Lido, Aave and ERC-20 steps persist as a recurring intent. This video, by its own description, does not show that recurring job actually running.

## Try it yourself

Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet. Ask Aomi to plan a multi-step sequence involving Lido, Aave and a token transfer. If it asks for scheduling details, answer from what you actually want.

Amounts, routes and quotes in the recording are examples. Yours will differ, so read the simulation before you sign.
