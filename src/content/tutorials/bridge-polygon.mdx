This page is based on the official video title and description, plus Aomi's public docs. I could not get captions for this recording, so the exact on-screen clicks were not independently verified.

The official description lists what happens in the recording:

- Bridged 50 USDC to Polygon through the PoS bridge
- Switched network to Polygon and confirmed that the deposit succeeded

To try a similar bridge today:

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet, as in the [Portal](https://aomi.dev/docs/trade/portal) guide.
2. Start on the source chain, then ask Aomi to bridge 50 USDC to Polygon through the PoS bridge.
3. Review the simulation and sign if you want to send it.
4. Switch the thread or wallet network to Polygon and confirm the deposit.

I did not re-run this bridge.
