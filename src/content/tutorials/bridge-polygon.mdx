Recorded by Aomi Labs: [Transact with Aomi - Bridge to Polygon](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d21mpys_9A0).

<Video src="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d21mpys_9A0" title="Transact with Aomi, Bridge to Polygon" />

What happens in the recording:

- Bridged 50 USDC to Polygon through the PoS bridge
- Switched network to Polygon and confirmed that the deposit succeeded

To try a similar bridge today:

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet, as in the [Portal](/guides/portal) guide.
2. Start on the source chain, then ask Aomi to bridge 50 USDC to Polygon through the PoS bridge.
3. Review the simulation and sign if you want to send it.
4. Switch the thread or wallet network to Polygon and confirm the deposit.
