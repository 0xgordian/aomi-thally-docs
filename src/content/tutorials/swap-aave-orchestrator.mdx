The request is to split 20 USDC between a staked-ETH token swap and an Aave deposit on Base. The orchestrator breaks the instruction into steps and prepares the actions for review. The amounts shown are examples, not a recommended strategy.

This is the same kind of "one instruction, two legs" flow Aomi has also described on X, with half going through a staked-ETH swap and half supplied to Aave.

## Try it yourself

Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet. Set the network to Base, then ask Aomi to take a USDC amount, swap half into staked ETH, and supply the other half to Aave. Review each prepared step before you sign.

Amounts, routes and quotes in the recording are examples. Yours will differ, so read the simulation before you sign.
