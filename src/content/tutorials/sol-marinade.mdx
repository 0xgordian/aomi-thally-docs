The session starts with a SOL-to-USDC swap and continues with a request to stake the remaining SOL through Marinade. It is a silent screen recording. Amounts and routes reflect the recorded session.

The [docs introduction](https://aomi.dev/docs) includes a follow-up in the same thread: "Now stake the rest with Marinade."

## Try it yourself

Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet. Set the thread to Solana, swap some SOL to USDC, then ask to stake the remaining SOL through Marinade. Review each step in your wallet before signing.

Amounts, routes and quotes in the recording are examples. Yours will differ. The portal doesn't fork-simulate Solana requests, so read the details in your wallet before you approve.
