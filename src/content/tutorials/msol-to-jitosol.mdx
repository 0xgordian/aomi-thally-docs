The recording shows route selection, simulation, wallet-approval instructions and a follow-up balance check. It is a silent product demo, and it is not a recommendation to hold either asset.

Aomi's [docs introduction](https://aomi.dev/docs) uses the same example: "Move my entire mSOL position over to JitoSOL," routed through Jupiter, with simulation passed and the transaction staged for approval.

## Try it yourself

Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev) and connect a wallet. Set the thread to Solana, then ask Aomi to move an mSOL position into JitoSOL. The portal docs note that Solana requests are signed and sent without a fork simulation, so review the transaction details in your wallet.

Amounts, routes and quotes in the recording are examples. Yours will differ. The portal doesn't fork-simulate Solana requests, so read the details in your wallet before you approve.
