Khalani is an intent network that handles cross-chain swaps and bridges, integrated as an Aomi App hosted on Aomi's AI cloud. The Khalani team gets an AI bot that talks to their trading backend, and Aomi is a distribution channel for that capability.

To try a hosted App like Khalani from the portal:

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev).
2. Use the packages catalog or app selector described in the [Portal](/guides/portal) guide.
3. If a Khalani app is installed or available, ask it to prepare a cross-chain swap or bridge, then review the simulation and sign in your wallet.

If Khalani isn't in your packages catalog, the App may have been renamed or removed.
