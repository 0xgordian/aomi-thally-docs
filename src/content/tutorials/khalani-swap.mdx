This page is based on the official video title and description, plus Aomi's public docs. I could not get captions for this recording, so the exact on-screen clicks were not independently verified.

The official description says Khalani is an intent network that handles cross-chain swaps and bridges, integrated as an Aomi App hosted on Aomi's AI cloud. The Khalani team gets an AI bot that talks to their trading backend, and Aomi is a distribution channel for that capability.

I do not have a public Khalani-specific command list. To try a hosted app from the portal:

1. Open [chat.aomi.dev](https://chat.aomi.dev).
2. Use the packages catalog or app selector described in the [Portal](https://aomi.dev/docs/trade/portal) guide.
3. If a Khalani app is installed or available, ask it to prepare a cross-chain swap or bridge, then review the simulation and sign in your wallet.

I could not verify whether Khalani is still listed in today's catalog.
